# Stoffverkauf Admin Panel - User Manual

Welcome to the Stoffverkauf E-Commerce Admin Panel. This manual will guide you through all the essential features of the admin dashboard to help you manage your store effectively.



---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Panel

To access the admin panel, navigate to `/admin/login` on your store's domain.

![Admin Login Screenshot](./screenshots/admin_login.png)

1. Enter your **Admin Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Login** to access the dashboard.
3. If you ever need to return to the shop or log out, you can use the **Back to Shop** or **Logout** buttons at the bottom of the sidebar.

---

## 2. Admin Sidebar Navigation

The admin panel features a collapsible sidebar on the left side of the screen.

![Sidebar Navigation](./screenshots/admin_sidebar.png)

- **Collapse/Expand**: Click the arrow icon at the top right of the sidebar to collapse it and save screen space.
- **Navigation Links**: Click on any section (Dashboard, Products, Orders, etc.) to navigate to its respective management page.

---

## 3. Dashboard (Übersicht)

The Dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of your store's performance.

![Dashboard Overview](./screenshots/admin_dashboard.png)

**Key Features:**
- View quick statistics like Total Sales, Active Orders, and New Customers.
- Quick links to recent orders or low-stock alerts.

---

## 4. Products Management (Produkte)

The Products section allows you to manage your entire inventory.

![Products Page](./screenshots/admin_products.png)

**How to Use:**
- **Add Product**: Click the "Add Product" button to create a new item. Fill in the title, description, price, stock, and upload images.
- **Edit/Delete**: Use the action buttons next to any existing product to update its details or remove it from the store.
- **Search & Filter**: Find products quickly using the search bar.

---

## 5. Categories (Kategorien)

Organize your products into categories for easier navigation on the frontend.

![Categories Page](./screenshots/admin_categories.png)

**How to Use:**
- Create main categories and subcategories.
- Assign products to specific categories so customers can filter by them.

---

## 6. Orders (Bestellungen) & Returns (Retouren)

Manage customer purchases and process returns efficiently.



**How to Use:**
- **View Orders**: See a list of all recent orders with their payment and fulfillment statuses.
- **Update Status**: Change the order status (e.g., Processing, Shipped, Delivered).
- **Handle Returns**: Navigate to the **Returns** tab to process refunded or exchanged items.

---

## 7. Customers (Kunden)

Keep track of registered users and their purchase history.



**How to Use:**
- View a list of all registered customers.
- Click on a customer to view their contact information and order history.
- Block or delete problematic accounts if necessary.

---

## 8. Analytics (Analyse)

Track your store's growth and sales trends.



**How to Use:**
- View detailed charts of revenue over time.
- Identify top-selling products and peak purchasing times.
- Export reports for your accounting.

---

## 9. Marketing & Content (Inhalte)

Run promotional campaigns and update store pages.



**How to Use:**
- **Marketing**: Create discount coupons, set up sales events, and manage newsletter subscribers.
- **Content**: Edit dynamic pages on your storefront (like About Us, Privacy Policy) without touching the code.

---

## 10. Feedback, Integrations & Settings

Manage external connections and global store settings.



**How to Use:**
- **Feedback**: Review customer feedback, contact form messages, or product reviews.
- **Integrations**: Connect third-party services (e.g., payment gateways, shipping providers).
- **Settings**: Configure store name, currency, tax rates, and admin notification preferences.
